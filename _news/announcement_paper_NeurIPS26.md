Five papers are accepted by **NeurIPS 2026**. One scales **quantum bandits to over 1000 arms** on IBM quantum hardware by tackling **memory decoherence**. The other attains the **quantum speedup** with only **log\*(n)+1 rounds of adaptivity**. The other three cover multi-agent learning in matching markets, adversarial attacks on bandits, and fused reward and dueling feedback. Many thanks to all my collaborators!
